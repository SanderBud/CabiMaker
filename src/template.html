A quick shelf-style layout with the long side of each piece along the sheet length. Use it to estimate how many sheets to buy; a panel shop may nest tighter.

  • The two outer side panels run the full cabinet height. Top, bottom and shelves fit between them.
  • Each shelf between two rows is one continuous piece across the full inner width.
  • Column dividers stand between two horizontal panels and are sunk into a groove (dado) in both. Their length is the opening height plus twice the sink. Columns in different rows do not have to line up.
  • The back panel is screwed onto the back and covers the full width and height. Sides, top, bottom, shelves and dividers are therefore as deep as the cabinet minus the back panel.
  • Drawers are plain boxes with the front flush with the cabinet. The drawer margin is taken off every side and off the back.
  • Drawer sides run the full drawer depth; the drawer front and back fit between them. The bottom sits under the whole box.
  • The Python engine is cabinet.py; run python cabinet.py config.json for the same cut list in a terminal.
